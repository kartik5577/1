# 🎓 Vectonix Classes - The Complete Master Guide

This guide is designed for both **Administrators** (Teachers/Owners) and **Students**. It explains how to fully utilize the portal for a seamless e-learning experience.

---

## 🏗️ Part 1: Administrator Guide (Teachers)

Your Admin Panel is the cockpit of your academy. Access it via the **Admin** link in the navigation bar.

### 1. Dashboard Overview
The dashboard gives you a bird's-eye view of your business:
- **Total Revenue**: Tracks every rupee earned.
- **Active Students**: Count of all registered users.
- **Recent Sales**: A live feed of latest purchases.

### 2. Managing Courses
Courses are your primary products. They act as "Folders" that can contain Notes and Lectures.
- **Tip**: Set a high-quality cover image to attract students.
- **Pricing**: You can make courses **Free** to act as lead magnets, or **Paid** for revenue.

### 3. Secure Notes & Curriculum
Vectonix Classes uses a **Secure Content Delivery Network**:
- **PDF Security**: When you upload or link a PDF, it is served via a custom viewer that prevents right-clicking, printing, or downloading.
- **Units**: Use the **"Units"** button on any note to break down a notebook into chapters. Each unit can have its own PDF and a set of **Previews** (images) for students to see before they buy.

### 4. Video Lectures
- **YouTube Integration**: Simply paste a YouTube link (Public or Unlisted).
- **Coming Soon**: You can list lectures as "Coming Soon" to build hype for an upcoming series.

### 5. Live Classes (The "Real-Time" Classroom)
Live classes are our standout feature.
- **Internal Room**: No need for Zoom or Google Meet. Use our built-in **Vectonix Live Bridge**. 
- **Teacher Controls**:
  - You are the Moderator.
  - Students enter muted.
  - You can use the **Face-to-Face Chat** to answer questions.
- **Joining**: Once you click **"Start Class"**, students see a "Live Now" pulsing button on their dashboard.

### 6. Preparation for Launch (Danger Zone)
Before you give this site to real students:
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Find the **Danger Zone**.
3. Click **"Clear All Training/Test Data"**.
4. This wipes the database of all "Physics" test data, leaving you with a clean academy.

## 👨‍🎓 Part 2: Student Guide

### 1. Your Personal Dashboard
After logging in with Google, you'll see:
- **My Courses**: Your personalized library of purchased content.
- **Live Now**: A notification bar that appears the moment your teacher starts a class.

### 2. Learning Experience
- **Interactive PDF Viewer**: Study units one by one. Use the "Dual-Mode" to keep a video open while reading notes.
- **Virtual Classroom**: Join live sessions with high-quality video and low-latency chat.

### 3. Purchasing Content
- Use the **Archive (Catalog)** to find new materials.
- Add items to your **Cart** and checkout using **Razorpay**.
- **Payment Success**: Your content is unlocked instantly—no manual verification needed.
